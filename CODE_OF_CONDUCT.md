# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want OpenBOLA to be a welcoming, technically rigorous community. Treat
people with respect, assume good faith, and focus criticism on the code,
evidence, and safety impact rather than on individuals.

Community members are expected to:

- communicate constructively and include reproducible evidence where possible;
- respect privacy and never publish credentials, customer data, or unauthorized
  target details;
- keep all testing within explicit written authorization;
- accept review, correction, and scope boundaries without harassment.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
publishing secrets or private test evidence, unsolicited scanning, credential
attacks, destructive testing, and attempts to bypass this project's safety
controls.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Security reports
must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue. Maintainers may
remove content, request changes, close discussions, or restrict participation
when needed to protect people, systems, or the project.

Retaliation against a good-faith report is not acceptable. We will handle
reports as confidentially as practical and share details only when necessary to
respond.
